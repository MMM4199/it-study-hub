🧭 The Big Picture

Information Technology (IT) is the use of computers, software, networks and digital systems to store, process, protect and share information.

🖥️
1. Birth of computers
Huge machines such as ENIAC performed calculations. Early computers could fill a whole room.
🌐
2. Commercial Internet
Computers became connected, allowing information to travel around the world.
📱
3. Smartphones
Computing became portable and available almost everywhere.
☁️
4. Cloud computing
Computing power, storage and software became services delivered through the Internet.
Exam order: Computer → Internet → Smartphone → Cloud

⚡ Key Concepts

📈

Moore's Law

Transistors on a chip roughly double every two years. This helped computing power increase dramatically.

☁️

Cloud

Remote servers provide storage, software and computing through the Internet.

📍

Edge computing

Data is processed near or on the device itself, reducing delay.

🤖

AI

Computers learn patterns and make predictions or decisions using data.

🌍 Five Social Changes

📲 SNS

Connect, post and share information rapidly.

🛒 E-commerce

Buying and selling through the Internet.

🏠 Remote work

Working from home or another remote location online.

🎓 Online learning

Classes and study materials delivered online.

💳 Cashless payment

Paying with cards, mobile apps or QR codes without cash.

🚀 Emerging Technologies

🚗 Autonomous driving

AI uses cameras and sensors to drive a vehicle without human operation.

🕶️ AR

Overlays digital information on real-world images.

🥽 VR

Immerses users in a computer-generated virtual space.

⚛️ Quantum computing

Uses quantum mechanics to solve certain difficult calculations more efficiently.
